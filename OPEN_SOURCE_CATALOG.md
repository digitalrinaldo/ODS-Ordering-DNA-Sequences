## 🏛️ Formative Engineering Chapters & Mentors (Deep-Dive Chronicles)

* ✈️ **[Chapter I: Aviation Simulations International (ASI) & Dr. Everett S. Joline (`digiorgiolabs.com/asi`)](https://digiorgiolabs.com/asi)** — Discrete-event airport surface & airspace simulations (`ASM-3`, `AM2D`, `AM3D`) and early computer-generated animations for the FAA, NASA & global airports in Huntington, Long Island.
* 🏭 **[Chapter II: MDDC (Management Decisions Development Corp.) — Ralph Hodges & George Beine (`digiorgiolabs.com/mddc`)](https://digiorgiolabs.com/mddc)** — Pioneering 24×7 computerized paper-mill roll tracking & wrapping-line control on minicomputers and microprocessors in Fairfield, Ohio (acquired by **Measurex Corporation** in 1985).
* ⚡ **[Chapter III: Industrial Programming, Inc. (IPI), Bernie Mushinsky & MTOS (`digiorgiolabs.com/mtos`)](https://digiorgiolabs.com/mtos)** — Serving as Ada Product Manager for `MTOS` (1976 `MTOS-80/68` through 16-CPU symmetric multiprocessor `MTOS-86MP`, `MTOS-68K` & `MTOS-UX`), mapping concurrent Ada Tasking & Rendezvous onto multiprocessor real-time kernels.
* 🌙 **[Chapter IV: Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) — Intel SBC 8080, Dr. Longini, Bruce McWilliams & the LEM (`digiorgiolabs.com/cmu`)](https://digiorgiolabs.com/cmu)** — Producing 3 standalone products from the Intel SBC 8080 work (Medical Oximeter, 8080 Disassembler & 8080 Floating-Point Package), plotting transcendental equation solutions on the Data General Nova 840 for PROM hematocrit/saturation lookup tables, and flying the LEM simulator at 3 AM.
* 🃏 **[Chapter V: Inventor Collaborations with A. James "Jim" Smith, Jr. (`digiorgiolabs.com/jim-smith`)](https://digiorgiolabs.com/jim-smith)** — Building the online Blackjack Basic Strategy & Card-Counting Drills engine (`BSE` / [US Patent 5,895,048](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5895048A/en)) on Sun Labs Brazil and the `ProtectPasswords` / `SecureNumber` visual grid login system ([US 6,253,328](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6253328B1/en) & [US 6,571,336](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6571336B1/en)).
